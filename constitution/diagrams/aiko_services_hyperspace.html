HyperSpace & ProcessManager

A Composite pattern all the way down: a Dependency is a leaf, a Category is a container that is also a Dependency, and HyperSpace is a Category that spans a whole graph. On disk that becomes a symlink tree over a content-addressed store. The LifeCycleManager seam is drawn dashed — the design intends HyperSpace and ProcessManager to be LifeCycleManagers, but neither is wired to it in v0.6.
